Plumber in Williston, Minnetonka, MN

Williston is the residential area along Williston Road south of Highway 7 in Minnetonka. This page is about plumbing help for those streets, which mix midcentury homes with newer infill houses. The Glen Lake page covers the shopping corridor at the road's north end.

Williston Road and the streets off it

A Homes.com neighborhood profile places Williston south of Highway 7, with mostly split-level and ranch homes, a median build year of 1965 and lots of about half an acre or more. A 2015 Star Tribune article described the single-family homes along Williston Road as tree-lined.

The Minnetonka Historical Society notes that the Carpenter and Aylor families farmed on Williston Road near Minnetonka Boulevard after 1860. Nothing we found gives a formal boundary for the neighborhood, so this page works from the road itself.

A 2016 planning review shows the area is still changing

The minutes cover a proposed four-lot development called Williston Woods West at 5431 Williston Road, with houses of about 4,400 square feet. Commissioners noted that Minnetonka lacks new single-family housing and recommended approval with a driveway-turnaround condition.

Neighbors raised runoff and flooding concerns on nearby Rainbow Drive, and the plan included a stormwater pond. The minutes record a recommendation and not what was finally built, so check county records for any specific address.

For a homeowner the takeaway is practical. Stormwater and grading are live topics along this road, so water in a lower level is worth a drainage check before assuming a pipe has failed.

Source: City of Minnetonka Planning Commission minutes, January 7, 2016.

Two kinds of Williston home

The first question differs by era.

Midcentury ranch or split-level

Ask where the sump pit, floor drain and main cleanout sit, and how old the water heater is.

Newer or rebuilt house

Ask the builder or previous owner which fixtures are original to the new work and what warranty remains.

Sump pumps, drains and water heaters

A sump pump that runs constantly, runs rarely when it should, or makes a grinding noise needs attention before the next heavy rain. See sump pump repair for how a failing pump is diagnosed.

A drain that slows after storms may point to groundwater reaching a floor drain, not a clog. A camera and a flow test separate the two. When a tank water heater is more than a few years from new and starts leaking, water heater replacement is the realistic path.

This is general plumbing guidance and not a claim about any Williston address.

When the water is coming from the street

If water is bubbling up at the road or in the right-of-way, it may be the public system. Williston Road is described in the 2016 minutes as busy, so keep clear of traffic and call Minnetonka Public Works at 952-988-8400.

Before you call about a Williston plumbing problem

A short checklist saves time.

Which level

Say whether the problem is in a lower level, the main floor or outside.

Pump status

If a sump pump is involved, say whether it is running, silent or noisy.

Recent rain

Mention heavy rain in the last day or two, because it changes the likely cause.

Photos

Take one wide shot and one close shot before you call.
