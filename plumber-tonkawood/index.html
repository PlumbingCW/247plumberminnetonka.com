Plumber in Tonkawood, Minnetonka, MN

Tonkawood takes its name from Tonkawood Road, west of the Highway 7 and I-494 interchange in Minnetonka. City records show the Tonkawood subdivision was platted in 1910, and homes there now range from older ramblers to newer houses.

What city records say about Tonkawood

A city planning packet from March 2017 states that the Tonkawood subdivision was platted in 1910. The same packet covers 3808 Tonkawood Road, a single-level rambler with a tuck-under garage that the owners proposed to replace with a new house, and it notes that surrounding lots are zoned for single-family use.

The Minnetonka Historical Society also names Tonkawood Road as the western edge of one of two large residential subdivisions platted by 1913.

A plat date tells you when lots were drawn, not when your house or its pipes went in. Ask for your own records before drawing conclusions about age.

Source: City of Minnetonka Planning Commission packet, March 2, 2017, and Minnetonka Historical Society.

New houses beside original ones

City records show a three-home redevelopment called Tonkawood Farms Third Addition at 15014 Highwood Drive. The council approved it in November 2020 and reviewed the final plat in October 2021.

When rebuilt houses sit next to original ones, the plumbing in neighboring homes can be a generation apart. Treat each address separately. If you bought or inherited a Tonkawood house, ask what was replaced and when, and keep the records.

For older sections, a sewer camera inspection shows the line's real condition. For a house with several bathrooms, a water heater installation plan should account for how many fixtures run at once.

If a toilet or tub backs up

These steps limit damage while you arrange help.

  1. Stop using water

    Do not flush or run the washer. More water only adds to the backup.

  2. Check other drains

    If a lower-level drain bubbles when an upper fixture runs, the main line is involved.

  3. Protect floors

    Move rugs and set towels around the affected drain.

  4. Call with details

    Say which fixtures are affected. Drain cleaning is the usual first step, and a camera follows if it returns.
