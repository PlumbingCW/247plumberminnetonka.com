About Minnetonka 24/7 Plumbing

Minnetonka 24/7 Plumbing is a round-the-clock phone line that connects people with licensed plumbers in Minnetonka, Hopkins, Wayzata, Eden Prairie, Plymouth and Excelsior.

Call (952) 866-9120

What this line is

Plumbing problems do not keep office hours. A pipe splits at two in the morning, a main line backs up on a holiday, a water heater starts leaking while you are at work. This business exists to answer that call, any hour, and get a licensed plumber moving toward your address.

The business address is 14525 Hwy 7, Minnetonka, MN 55345, in the Hwy 7 area of west Minnetonka. Dispatch runs by phone. There is no online booking page and no message queue, because someone with water on the floor needs a person, not a form. The plumbers dispatched work to the Minnesota Plumbing Code, Minnesota Rules chapter 4714, and meet the licensing standards set by the Minnesota Department of Labor and Industry.

How dispatch works

Every call follows the same few steps, whether it is a stuck faucet or a flooded basement.

  1. A person answers

    The dispatcher listens to what is happening, asks where the water is and what you have already shut off, and notes the address and the type of building.

  2. The job is matched

    The call is matched to a licensed plumber who handles that kind of work, such as drain clearing, water heaters, sewer lines or sump pumps.

  3. The price comes first

    Before any work starts, the plumber explains what they found and what it will cost. You can say yes, ask questions or decline.

  4. The work is done and checked

    The plumber completes the repair, tests it and cleans up the work area. Same-day appointments are available when the schedule allows for work that is not an emergency.

What licensed and insured means

In Minnesota, plumbing work in communities with more than 5,000 people requires a plumbing contractor license. The people doing the work must be licensed journeyworker or master plumbers, registered unlicensed plumbers or registered apprentices, and the company's responsible individual must hold an active master plumber license.

Insured means the plumbing company carries insurance for its work. If you want to see proof of coverage on a specific job, ask the plumber who arrives, since that is a question about their company directly.

You do not have to take anyone's word for a license. The Minnesota Department of Labor and Industry runs a public Check a License lookup. Search the company name or the plumber's name there before you agree to work, whether you called this line or another one.

What the line handles

The services behind the phone range from urgent repairs to planned projects.

  • Emergencies such as burst pipes, sewer backups, overflowing fixtures and leaking water heaters.
  • Drain and sewer work, including drain cleaning, hydro jetting and camera inspections.
  • Water heaters, from repair to new installation.
  • Planned work like repiping, fixture installation and basement waterproofing.
  • Commercial calls from shops, offices and apartment buildings, handled through commercial plumbing.

Where the line works

The line serves Minnetonka and nearby towns: Hopkins, Wayzata, Eden Prairie, Plymouth and Excelsior. The service area page explains how each town differs in housing, water system and permits.

Those differences matter. Minnetonka has older single-family stock from 1950 to 1970 and many apartment and condo buildings, and each town around it has its own city utility and its own permit office. A plumber who knows the area asks the right questions the first time.

What this business does not do

It does not promise an arrival time, because traffic on Hwy 7 and I-494, weather and the number of calls at that moment all change how fast a plumber can get to you. It does not list prices on the website, because a fair price depends on seeing the problem.

It does not diagnose health effects of water, and it does not decide for you whether work needs a permit. For permits, ask the City of Minnetonka building department or the building office in your own town. It also does not publish reviews, awards or other claims it cannot back up here, so the facts on these pages are limited to what can be checked.
