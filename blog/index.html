Minnetonka Plumbing Guides and Tips

Practical plumbing guides for Minnetonka homeowners, written to help you understand a problem before you decide what to do about it.

Call (952) 866-9120

What the guides cover

The guides answer the questions people ask when something in the house stops working. How big a tankless water heater do I need? What is a toilet flange? What does a hydro jetter actually do inside a sewer pipe?

Each one explains the topic in plain terms, points out where a repair is realistic for a handy homeowner and where a licensed plumber is the safer choice. Local notes tie the topic back to Minnetonka and nearby towns, using public city information. Each guide points back to the Minnesota Plumbing Code, Minnesota Rules chapter 4714, whenever a rule matters, and tells you to check local standards for permits.

How to use a guide

A few habits make the guides more useful.

  • Read the whole guide before touching a tool. Water, gas and electricity are all in play around a water heater.
  • Check the local note at the end for permit and utility details, then confirm with your city's building department.
  • Look for the signs that mean stop and call, like gas smells, rising water or a wet electrical panel.
  • Use the cost factors to compare quotes, since the same job can differ by access and parts.
  • If you are mid-problem, call the 24/7 line now and read later.

What the guides will not do

They are not a substitute for a licensed plumber, and they do not quote prices. Permit rules vary by city, so each guide tells you whom to ask instead of guessing. If a guide describes a repair you are not sure you can do, that doubt is a good reason to call.
