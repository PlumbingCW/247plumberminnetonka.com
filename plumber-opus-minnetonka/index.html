Plumber in the Opus Area, Minnetonka, MN

Opus is a mixed-use area in Minnetonka near Highway 169, Highway 62 and Shady Oak Road. It is not a typical residential subdivision. The city says about 4,000 people live there and more than 15,000 work there, so plumbing questions here lean commercial and multifamily.

Call (952) 866-9120

What the city says about Opus

The study describes Opus 2 as a mixed-use community of housing, employment centers, a one-way road network and about six miles of off-road trails. It says the area was established nearly 40 years ago and is being revitalized mainly because a light rail line and station are coming.

The same study says new development would add more than 1,000 housing units, over half of them affordable. It gives no opening date for the line and no formal boundary for Opus.

For building owners this means older commercial buildings and new multifamily construction sit side by side, and their plumbing needs differ.

Source: City of Minnetonka Opus public space study.

How commercial and multifamily calls differ

A commercial call usually involves more than one fixture or tenant, so the first step is to define scope. Say how many fixtures are affected, whether the problem repeats, and who is authorized to approve work on the account.

A water heater replacement in a building with many users is sized to how many people draw hot water at once. A drain problem in a shared stack needs the property manager involved. The commercial plumbing page describes the work this line takes.

For any larger project, ask about permits before work starts. This is a general expectation for commercial plumbing and not a claim about a specific Opus project.

Check a contractor's license, including ours

The Minnesota Department of Labor and Industry licenses plumbing contractors and offers an online license lookup. Before any commercial or large residential job, ask for the contractor's license number and check it there. The owner of this website should supply their own license details on request.
