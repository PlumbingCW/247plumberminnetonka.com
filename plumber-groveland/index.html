Plumber in Groveland, Minnetonka, MN

Groveland is an established residential area in central Minnetonka, known through Groveland Elementary and the city's Gro Tonka Park. This page covers plumbing help for Groveland homes and says plainly which local facts could and could not be verified.

Where Groveland sits, and what it is not

A Homes.com neighborhood profile places Groveland in central Minnetonka. The Minnetonka Historical Society says the Chowen, Shaver, Gray and Ogin families had settled the Groveland area before 1860.

Groveland Elementary is a Minnetonka public school, and the district describes its community as Minnetonka and Woodland together. Woodland is a separate residential community on Lake Minnetonka's south shore and is not part of this page.

No source we found draws a formal Groveland boundary, so this page uses landmarks and road names instead.

Housing and lot size

The Homes.com profile lists mostly midcentury ranches and split-levels, a median build year of 1959, and lots from a quarter acre to a full acre with a median of 17,424 square feet. It is a real estate source, so treat the figures as a rough sketch.

Here is a general plumbing point and not a finding about any Groveland house. On a larger lot the pipe run between the house and the street can be long. Before anyone digs, a camera inspection and a line locate show where the problem is.

If roots are the suspect, hydro jetting clears roots from a line but does not repair a break. A camera tells you which one you have.

What to do when a main drain backs up

Use these steps while you wait to talk to a plumber.

  1. Stop adding water

    Hold off on laundry, dishwashers and long showers until the line is cleared.

  2. Check other fixtures

    If the lowest drain gurgles or backs up first, the main line is the likely problem.

  3. Find the cleanout

    Note where the cleanout cap sits, but do not open it unless you know how to manage what comes out.

  4. Call and describe

    Say which fixtures are affected and whether anything is overflowing. Drain cleaning is usually the first step.

Street-wide water problems and the public system

KSTP reported in September 2024 that Minnetonka listens to roughly 3,500 fire hydrants for leaks, and that Public Works finds 12 to 15 leaks a year. The city lists water, sewer, stormwater and utility billing under Public Works at 952-988-8400.

If low pressure or cloudy water affects your whole street, ask a neighbor before you assume the problem is inside your house. A street-wide issue belongs to the public system, and the city can tell you.

Source: KSTP, September 25, 2024, and Minnetonka Public Works.
