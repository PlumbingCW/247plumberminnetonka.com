Urgent

If water is moving right now

Take thirty seconds to cut the damage before you dial. It makes the call simpler and the repair smaller.

  1. Close the main water valve, usually on the basement wall where the line enters the house.
  2. Switch off the water heater at the breaker or turn the gas control to pilot.
  3. Stop using drains and toilets if sewage is coming up.
  4. Move electronics, rugs and boxes away from wet areas, and stay clear of outlets near standing water.
  5. If you smell gas, leave the house and call 911 before anything else.

What to tell the dispatcher

A clear description lets the dispatcher match your call to the right licensed plumber on the first try.

  • Your street address and the town, plus the building type: house, townhome, condo or commercial space.
  • What is happening and where, such as water from a ceiling, a toilet overflowing or no hot water.
  • What you have already done, like shutting the main valve or turning off the water heater.
  • Whether water is touching electrical items or if you smell gas.
  • A callback number in case the call drops, and your role: owner, tenant or property manager.
  • Anything about access: a locked basement, a gate or a building entry code.

The line covers Minnetonka and the towns around it: Hopkins to the east, Wayzata on the lake's north shore, Excelsior on the south shore, Eden Prairie to the south and Plymouth to the north.

Not on the list? Call anyway. Places around Lake Minnetonka often can be served from the same line, and the dispatcher will tell you straight if an address is out of range.

What happens after you call

The dispatcher assigns a licensed plumber, and you get the next step. The plumber explains the problem and states a clear price before work starts. For routine requests, same-day appointments are offered when available.

No one on the line will promise an exact arrival time. Hwy 7 and I-494 traffic and the night's call volume change too much, and a made-up window helps nobody. If you want more on how the service works, the about page explains dispatch in detail.

About water damage cleanup

After a burst pipe or a backup, you may need drying, carpet removal or mold checks. Those are separate trades from plumbing. In general, call your insurer early, photograph everything and keep wet materials until the adjuster has seen them. The plumber's job is to stop the water and fix the pipe.

Planned work and questions

Replacing a water heater, adding a fixture or planning a repipe can be scheduled by phone too. Read the services overview first if you want to know what the work involves, then call with questions.

For plumbing itself, a phone call is quicker and keeps a person on the other end of the problem. The email address stays for general business questions.
