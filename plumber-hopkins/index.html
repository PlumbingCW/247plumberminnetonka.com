Sixty-year-old houses and the drains under them

Hopkins housing is old, and the city's own housing plan says so. About 60 percent of owner-occupied homes are at least 60 years old. That age predicts the calls we dispatch for: clay and cast iron house drains with root-filled joints, galvanized supply lines narrowed by rust, and original traps that clog under a heavy load. A drain camera shows which one you have before anyone cuts a floor.

Renters live in a different era. The same plan says two-thirds of renter-occupied housing went up between 1960 and 1989, so many apartment buildings run cast iron stacks and shared building drains. When two units back up together, the blockage sits in the shared line, not in a sink. Managers who say that on the first call save a visit spent cabling the wrong fixture.

Hopkins issues plumbing permits through its Inspections Division, and the work is held to the standards of the Minnesota Plumbing Code, Minnesota Rules chapter 4714.

Clearing the clog first, repairing the sewer when the camera says so

Most Hopkins backups start as a clog and stay a clog. A cable or a hydro jetter clears grease, scale and roots, and a drain cleaning visit is the usual first move for a slow tub or a gurgling floor drain near downtown in 55343.

Some do not stay clogs. If roots return within months, or the camera shows a sagging section that holds water or a cracked clay joint, cleaning only buys time. That is when sewer line repair enters, by spot repair, lining or replacement, depending on what the picture shows. In older blocks of 55305, a repeat clog is often a failing pipe, which is why the camera comes first.

Keep rain out of the sanitary line. Hopkins stormwater drains through the storm sewer to the Minnehaha Creek and Nine Mile Creek watersheds, a separate system from the sanitary sewer. A downspout or sump discharge tied to the wrong one can overload a house drain in a storm, so tell the plumber where your gutters and sump pump empty.

Why the tap runs brown, and when to worry

Hopkins runs its own water system from three wells about 500 feet deep. The city filters iron, but says some remains and coats the inside of the water main pipes. Hydrant flushing, main breaks, service leaks and fire use can knock those deposits loose, and brown or rusty water shows up at your tap.

Run the cold tap for a few minutes until it clears. Skip the hot tap, which would pull sediment into your water heater. If the water clears, you do not need a plumber. If it stays brown, or only one fixture shows it, the cause may be your own pipes, and that is a call for the 24/7 line.

The city also states that its water measures 21 grains of hardness. Expect scale on faucet aerators and inside tank heaters, and a water heater repair call often traces back to that buildup.

Main Street buildings inside the historic district

The Hopkins Commercial Historic District runs along Main Street from 8th to 11th Avenues and joined the National Register in 2022. Buildings there are old, and their basements hold floor drains and patched lines from several decades. For a shop or restaurant owner, the useful step is to find the floor drains and the water shutoff now, since a plumber starts at exactly those points.

A listing is a recognition, not a plumbing rule, so ask the City of Hopkins whether it changes anything for your project. The 24/7 line takes commercial plumbing calls the same way it takes house calls.

Before we arrive at your Hopkins home

Four quick steps limit the damage while the plumber is on the way.

Find the main shutoff

In a mid-century Hopkins house the main valve usually sits in the basement where the water line enters. Old gate valves can be stiff, so turn slowly and stop if it resists.

Kill power to wet areas

If a basement floor drain is backing up, switch off the breakers for the basement outlets and stay off wet carpet. Leave the furnace alone if water is near it.

Clear the area

Move boxes off the basement floor and away from the drain. In an apartment building, warn the neighbors below and make sure the utility room is open.

Call the 24/7 line
